Who We Are

Lithium-ion Battery Manufacturers in India

iNextGen Power Batteries Private Limited is a new-age lithium-ion battery company, incorporated in June 2026 and headquartered in Roorkee, Uttarakhand. We are building India's clean-energy future by delivering safe, dependable and long-lasting battery solutions.

Our focus is on powering electric mobility, renewable energy storage and everyday backup needs. Every pack we build is designed around quality cells, a smart Battery Management System (BMS) and careful testing — so our customers get batteries that are practical, scalable and built to last.
